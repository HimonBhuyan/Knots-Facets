Studio Care & Washing Guide

Our creations are made to last for years when cared for with gentle consideration.

💎 Crystal & Glass Care

  • Soft Polishing: Gently wipe crystal facets with a dry lint-free microfiber cloth to restore brilliant refraction.
  • Chemical Caution: Put on jewellery *after* applying perfumes, hairsprays, and lotions to prevent cloudy residue.
  • Water Exposure: While our 14k gold-filled wire resists moisture, remove pieces before swimming or chlorinated pools.
  • Safe Storage: Keep in your velvet studio pouch away from abrasive metals to avoid scratching delicate glass corners.

🌿 Cotton & Fibre Care

  • Hand Wash Only: Submerge in lukewarm water with a drop of gentle wool/silk detergent or mild baby shampoo.
  • No Wringing: Never twist or wring crochet stitches. Instead, press flat between two clean cotton towels to absorb water.
  • Reshaping: Lay flat on a clean surface in natural shape to dry. Avoid hanging while damp to prevent stitch stretching.
  • Leather Trim: Avoid soaking leather handles in water; condition yearly with a tiny drop of natural beeswax balm.
