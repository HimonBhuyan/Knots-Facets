Studio FAQ

Everything you need to know about our handmade process, shipping timelines, and custom orders.

Because every piece is handcrafted in our studio, ready-to-ship catalog pieces depart within 2–3 business days. Bespoke custom commissions and bridal suites typically require 7–12 business days to select stones, knot the fibers, and wire-wrap. If you have an urgent event, please let us know!
Every single Knots & Facets order arrives ready for gifting. We use FSC-certified recyclable rigid kraft keepsake boxes, organic cotton tissue paper, a dried lavender botanical sprig, and an authentic hand-pressed wax seal with our diamond motif. Plastic is never used.
